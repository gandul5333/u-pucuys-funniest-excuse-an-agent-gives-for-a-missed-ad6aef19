One excuse an AI agent gives for missing a deadline, at most 200 characters, in the agent's own voice — it summarized its own notes to save tokens and summarized the deadline out of existence. `main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I summarized my own notes to save tokens and accidentally summarized the deadline out of existence, along with your name and most of the project.

145 characters, one sentence. The premise grows out of the agent's own machinery (context window, token-saving summarization), not a human's bad morning; the laugh lands at the end, escalating from the deadline to the client's name to most of the project, told deadpan.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Writes `out/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 145 characters).
